# Regulatory Compliance Packs

## Pack Structure

- Jurisdiction profile (HIPAA/GDPR/local law)
- Data classification matrix
- Consent requirements
- Data subject rights process
- Incident notification obligations
- Cross-border transfer constraints

## Included Baseline Packs

- HIPAA readiness checklist (US)
- GDPR readiness checklist (EU)
- Local regulatory extension template
- Medical device scope decision log template

## Operational Notes

- Maintain versioned legal policy documents.
- Evidence folder should include signed audit snapshots and retention logs.
