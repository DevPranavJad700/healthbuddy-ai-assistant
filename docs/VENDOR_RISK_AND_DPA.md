# Vendor Risk and DPA Controls

## Minimum Controls for Model/API Providers

- Signed Data Processing Agreement (DPA)
- Security questionnaire and SOC2/ISO evidence where available
- Data retention and deletion guarantees
- Incident reporting SLA
- Subprocessor disclosure

## Vendor Risk Register Fields

- vendor_name
- service_scope
- data_types_transferred
- dpa_status
- risk_rating
- mitigation_plan
- review_date

## Review Cadence

- Initial onboarding review
- Quarterly risk reassessment
- Triggered reassessment on provider incident/change
