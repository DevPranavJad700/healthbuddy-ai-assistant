## 1. USPSTF Cancer Screening Matrix (Evidence Grades A & B)

Screening detects premalignant lesions and asymptomatic early-stage malignancies when curative treatment is most effective.

| Malignancy | Target Demographic | Screening Modality & Interval | Clinical Thresholds & Discontinuation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Colorectal Cancer (CRC)** | Adults aged 45 to 75 years (Grade A) | 1. Colonoscopy every 10 years (Gold standard)<br>2. Annual FIT (Fecal Immunochemical Test)<br>3. Stool DNA-FIT (Cologuard) every 3 years | Stop routinely at age 75. Adults 76–85 screened selectively based on overall health, prior screening, and life expectancy > 10 yrs. |
| **Breast Cancer** | Women aged 40 to 74 years (Grade B) | Biennial (every 2 years) screening digital mammography | Clinical breast exams alone not recommended. High-risk women (BRCA1/2 mutation or >20% lifetime risk) need annual MRI + mammogram starting at age 30. |
| **Cervical Cancer** | Women aged 21 to 65 years (Grade A) | • Ages 21–29: Cervical cytology (Pap smear) every 3 years.<br>• Ages 30–65: Either cytology every 3 yrs, high-risk HPV (hrHPV) alone every 5 yrs, or co-testing (Pap + hrHPV) every 5 yrs. | Discontinue after age 65 if adequate prior negative screening and no history of CIN 2+. ## 2. Cardiovascular & Metabolic Disease Prevention

### A. Lipid Screening & ASCVD Risk Assessment
- **Screening Interval**: Fasting or non-fasting lipid profile every 4 to 6 years in all adults aged 20 years and older without known cardiovascular disease; annual testing in high-risk individuals.
- **10-Year ASCVD Risk Categories (Pooled Cohort Equations)**:
  - **Low Risk (< 5%)**: Emphasize lifestyle modifications (DASH/Mediterranean diet, physical activity, smoking cessation).
  - **Borderline Risk (5% to < 7.5%)**: If risk enhancers are present (family history of premature ASCVD, metabolic syndrome, chronic kidney disease, inflammatory diseases), consider moderate-intensity statin.
  - **Intermediate Risk (7.5% to < 20%)**: Moderate-intensity statin therapy (reduces LDL-C by 30% to 49%). Consider Coronary Artery Calcium (CAC) scoring if treatment decision is uncertain. (CAC = 0 reclassifies to lower risk; CAC >= 100 or >= 75th percentile favors statin initiation).
  - **High Risk (>= 20%)**: High-intensity statin therapy (Atorvastatin 40–80 mg or Rosuvastatin 20–40 mg) targeting >= 50% LDL-C reduction.

### B. Hypertension Screening & Monitoring
- Annual blood pressure screening in adults aged 18 and older with normal BP (< 120/80 mmHg).
- Semi-annual screening for adults with risk factors or prior readings of 120–129 mmHg systolic.
- Home Blood Pressure Monitoring (HBPM) or 24-hour Ambulatory Blood Pressure Monitoring (ABPM) recommended to rule out "White Coat Hypertension" or identify "Masked Hypertension" before initiating lifelong pharmacotherapy.

### C. Type 2 Diabetes & Prediabetes Screening
- Screen all asymptomatic adults aged 35 to 70 years who are overweight or obese (BMI >= 25 kg/m2, or >= 23 kg/m2 in Asian Americans).
- Repeat testing every 3 years if normal.
- **Diagnostic Criteria**:
  - **Prediabetes**: Fasting Plasma Glucose 100–125 mg/dL (5.6–6.9 mmol/L) OR 2-hr Oral Glucose Tolerance Test 140–199 mg/dL OR HbA1c 5.7% to 6.4%.
  - **Overt Diabetes**: Fasting Plasma Glucose >= 126 mg/dL OR 2-hr OGTT >= 200 mg/dL OR HbA1c >= 6.5% (confirmed on repeat testing) OR random glucose >= 200 mg/dL with classic hyperglycemic symptoms (polyuria, polydipsia, unexplained weight loss).

## 3. Evidence-Based Lifestyle Medicine Protocols

### A. Dietary Guidelines: Mediterranean & DASH Frameworks
- **Mediterranean Diet Pattern**:
  - Emphasizes extra virgin olive oil as primary fat source, abundant vegetables, legumes, whole grains, nuts (walnuts, almonds), seeds, and moderate intake of fatty cold-water fish (salmon, mackerel, sardines) rich in Omega-3 EPA/DHA.
  - Proven by the PREDIMED trial to reduce major cardiovascular events by 30%.
- **DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension) Pattern**:
  - Target sodium: < 1,500 mg/day (ideal) or < 2,300 mg/day (standard).
  - Emphasizes potassium-rich foods (bananas, sweet potatoes, spinach, avocado: goal 4,700 mg/day elemental potassium in absence of CKD), magnesium, and dietary fiber (> 30 g/day).
  - Lowers systolic BP by an average of 8 to 14 mmHg in hypertensive individuals.

### B. Physical Activity Prescription (ACSM / CDC Guidelines)
- **Aerobic Conditioning**:
  - Minimum 150 to 300 minutes per week of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise (e.g., brisk walking at 3.5–4.0 mph, cycling at 10–12 mph, water aerobics), OR
  - 75 to 150 minutes per week of vigorous-intensity aerobic exercise (e.g., jogging, lap swimming, singles tennis), OR an equivalent combination.
- **Resistance / Muscle-Strengthening**:
  - Minimum 2 non-consecutive days per week involving all major muscle groups (legs, hips, back, abdomen, chest, shoulders, arms).
  - 8 to 12 repetitions per set at 60–80% of 1-Repetition Maximum (1-RM) to preserve skeletal muscle mass (counteracting sarcopenia) and enhance insulin receptor sensitivity.
- **Neuromotor / Balance Training**:
  - Minimum 3 days per week for older adults (aged >= 65) to reduce fall risk (Tai Chi, single-leg stands, tandem walking).

## 4. Adult Immunization Framework (CDC ACIP Guidelines)

| Vaccine | Indicated Cohort | Dosing Schedule & Frequency | Clinical Nuances |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Influenza (Inactivated or Recombinant)** | All individuals >= 6 months annually | 1 dose every autumn (September/October) | High-dose (Fluzone High-Dose) or Adjuvanted (Fluad) preferred for adults >= 65 years. |
| **COVID-19 (Updated Formulation)** | All individuals >= 6 months | 1 annual updated dose | Additional doses recommended for immunocompromised individuals. |
| **Pneumococcal (PCV20 or PCV15 followed by PPSV23)** | Adults >= 65 years, or aged 19–64 with chronic conditions (diabetes, heart disease, COPD, CKD) | Single dose of PCV20 (Prevnar 20), OR PCV15 followed by PPSV23 one year later | Protects against invasive Streptococcus pneumoniae pneumonia, bacteremia, and meningitis. |
| **Zoster (Recombinant, Shingrix)** | Adults >= 50 years; immunocompromised >= 19 years | 2-dose series: Dose 2 administered 2 to 6 months after Dose 1 | >90% efficacy in preventing Herpes Zoster and debilitating Postherpetic Neuralgia. Non-live vaccine. |
| **RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus)** | Adults >= 60 years | 1 single dose based on shared clinical decision-making | Strongly recommended for adults with cardiopulmonary disease or frail status. |
| **Tdap / Td (Tetanus, Diphtheria, Pertussis)** | All adults | Tdap once, then Td or Tdap booster every 10 years | 1 dose Tdap during every pregnancy (weeks 27–36) to confer passive neonatal pertussis immunity. |

## 5. Mental Health Screening & Stress Neurobiology

### Validated Clinical Screening Instruments
- **PHQ-9 (Patient Health Questionnaire-9)**:
  - Validated tool for screening and monitoring severity of Major Depressive Disorder.
  - Scores: 0–4 (None), 5–9 (Mild), 10–14 (Moderate), 15–19 (Moderately Severe), 20–27 (Severe).
  - Question 9 assesses suicidal ideation: ANY non-zero response triggers immediate clinical crisis intervention protocols.
- **GAD-7 (Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7)**:
  - Scores: 0–4 (Minimal), 5–9 (Mild), 10–14 (Moderate), 15–21 (Severe).
  - Score >= 10 has sensitivity and specificity > 80% for GAD.

### Sleep Architecture & Insomnia Interventions
- **Healthy Sleep Duration**: 7 to 9 hours of restorative sleep per 24-hour cycle for adults aged 18–64; 7 to 8 hours for adults >= 65.
- **Cognitive Behavioral Therapy for Insomnia (CBT-I)**:
  - First-line treatment for chronic insomnia, superior to pharmacotherapy long-term.
  - **Stimulus Control**: Use bed only for sleep and intimacy. Leave bed if unable to sleep after 20 minutes; engage in a quiet, low-light activity until sleepy.
  - **Sleep Restriction**: Match time in bed to actual total sleep time (minimum 5 hours), consolidating sleep efficiency to > 85% before gradually expanding time in bed.
  - **Circadian Phase Alignment**: Maintain consistent wake time 7 days a week, including weekends; obtain 15–30 minutes of natural outdoor sunlight within 1 hour of waking.
