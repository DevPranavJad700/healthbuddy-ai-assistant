SECTION A: CONDITION-SPECIFIC VARIANTS
======================================

A1) Diabetes-focused adaptations
- Exercise: prioritize post-meal walking (10 to 20 minutes), resistance work 2 to 3 times weekly, and reduced sedentary time.
- Diet: pair carbohydrates with protein/fiber, prefer whole grains/legumes, minimize sugary drinks.
- Sleep/stress: poor sleep and chronic stress can worsen glycemic control.
- Monitoring: track glucose patterns as advised by clinician, especially after new meal or activity routines.

A2) Hypertension-focused adaptations
- Exercise: 150+ minutes moderate cardio weekly plus 2 strength sessions.
- Diet: DASH-style pattern with vegetables, fruits, legumes, low-fat dairy, nuts; lower sodium intake.
- Sleep/stress: stress and sleep deprivation can elevate blood pressure.
- Caution: dizziness, severe headache, chest pain, or neurologic deficits need urgent evaluation.

A3) Obesity/weight-management adaptations
- Exercise: combine moderate cardio + progressive strength for fat loss and muscle retention.
- Diet: energy deficit with high-fiber foods and protein at meals to improve satiety.
- Sleep/stress: short sleep is associated with appetite dysregulation.
- Goal framing: even 5 to 10 percent weight reduction can improve blood pressure and glucose markers.

A4) PCOS-focused adaptations
- Exercise: regular aerobic plus strength training supports insulin sensitivity.
- Diet: emphasize low-glycemic, high-fiber meals with sufficient protein.
- Sleep/stress: stress management and adequate sleep can support symptom control.
- Clinical note: personalized treatment may include lifestyle plus medication.


SECTION B: PROGRESSION PLANS BY LEVEL
=====================================

B1) Exercise progression
Beginner (weeks 1 to 4):
- 20 to 30 min brisk walk, 4 to 5 days weekly.
- 2 short strength sessions: squat, push, hinge, pull, core.
- Daily mobility 5 to 10 min.

Intermediate (weeks 5 to 12):
- 30 to 45 min cardio, 4 to 5 days weekly.
- 2 to 3 strength sessions with gradual load progression.
- 1 interval session weekly if tolerated.

Advanced (maintenance/performance):
- Periodized training with aerobic base + intervals + structured strength.
- Include recovery days, deload weeks, and mobility/balance work.

B2) Sleep improvement progression
Beginner:
- Fixed wake time and caffeine cutoff.
- Screen-light reduction 60 min before bed.

Intermediate:
- Add pre-sleep routine (10 to 20 min): reading, breathing, stretching.
- Optimize room temperature and noise control.

Advanced:
- Track sleep consistency and triggers.
- Adapt routine for travel/shift changes while protecting anchor wake time.

B3) Balanced diet progression
Beginner:
- Plate method: half vegetables, quarter protein, quarter high-fiber carbs.
- Replace sugary drinks with water/unsweetened options.

Intermediate:
- Protein target at each meal.
- Meal-prep 2 to 3 times weekly and portion consistency.

Advanced:
- Data-informed adjustments based on labs, energy, training load, and goals.
- Fine-tune fiber quality, meal timing, and micronutrient adequacy.

SECTION D: RETRIEVAL FAQ VARIANTS
=================================

Q: I have high blood pressure. What is one safe starting routine?
A: Start with 25 to 30 minutes brisk walking most days, two light strength sessions weekly, and reduce sodium while monitoring blood pressure regularly.

Q: I have prediabetes. What should I do this week?
A: Do post-meal walks, switch sugary drinks to water, use high-fiber carbs with protein, and target consistent sleep.

Q: Give an intermediate plan for fat loss without muscle loss.
A: Use 4 to 5 cardio sessions weekly plus 2 to 3 progressive strength sessions and protein-focused meals with a moderate calorie deficit.

Q: I work shifts and cannot sleep properly. First 3 fixes?
A: Keep an anchor wake time, use blackout/light control, and follow a fixed pre-sleep routine with caffeine cutoff.
