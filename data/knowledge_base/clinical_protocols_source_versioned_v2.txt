HEALTHBUDDY CLINICAL PROTOCOLS SOURCE VERSIONED V2
=================================================

Protocol metadata
- Corpus version: hb-clinical-protocols-v2.0
- Clinical validation status: internal clinician-reviewed draft
- Source policy: each protocol block includes source family and version tag
- Scope: triage and first-line educational guidance only

SAFETY DISCLAIMER
- Educational support only. Not a diagnosis.
- Red-flag symptoms require in-person care.


PROTOCOL 1: ACUTE CHEST PAIN AND DYSPNEA
========================================
source_family: cardio_emergency_pathway
source_version: CEP-2026.1

Red flags:
- chest pain/pressure longer than 5 to 10 minutes
- shortness of breath, cold sweats, nausea, syncope, radiation to arm/jaw/back

Triage:
- emergency

Immediate guidance:
- call emergency services now
- avoid self-driving
- rest in seated position while awaiting help


PROTOCOL 2: ACUTE STROKE FAST SCREEN
====================================
source_family: neuro_emergency_pathway
source_version: NEP-2026.1

Red flags:
- face droop, arm weakness, speech difficulty, sudden focal neurologic deficit

Triage:
- emergency

Immediate guidance:
- call emergency services immediately
- document last-known-well time


PROTOCOL 3: HYPERTENSIVE CRISIS SCREEN
======================================
source_family: hypertension_pathway
source_version: HTP-2026.2

Red flags:
- blood pressure >= 180/120 with chest pain, neurologic signs, dyspnea, or confusion

Triage:
- emergency when target-organ symptoms are present
- urgent if severe elevation without target-organ symptoms

Immediate guidance:
- emergency pathway if red flags present
- same-day clinician assessment if no red flags but very high readings persist


PROTOCOL 4: DIABETIC CRISIS RED FLAGS
=====================================
source_family: diabetes_acute_pathway
source_version: DAP-2026.2

Red flags:
- persistent vomiting, abdominal pain, dehydration, fruity breath, rapid breathing, confusion

Triage:
- emergency

Immediate guidance:
- emergency evaluation for suspected DKA/HHS


PROTOCOL 5: SEVERE DEHYDRATION / GI LOSS
========================================
source_family: hydration_gastro_pathway
source_version: HGP-2026.1

Red flags:
- markedly reduced urination, confusion, persistent vomiting, inability to keep fluids, blood in stool

Triage:
- urgent to emergency depending on symptom severity

Immediate guidance:
- urgent in-person evaluation if persistent symptoms
- emergency services if confusion, collapse, or severe weakness


PROTOCOL 6: ASTHMA OR ALLERGIC RESPIRATORY FLARE
=================================================
source_family: respiratory_acute_pathway
source_version: RAP-2026.1

Red flags:
- severe breathlessness, wheeze not improving, inability to speak full sentences, cyanosis, anaphylaxis signs

Triage:
- emergency

Immediate guidance:
- emergency services if severe breathing compromise
- use prescribed rescue treatment if available while awaiting care


PROTOCOL 7: FEVER WITH HIGH-RISK FEATURES
==========================================
source_family: infectious_risk_pathway
source_version: IRP-2026.1

Red flags:
- fever with confusion, severe headache, neck stiffness, persistent vomiting, breathing difficulty

Triage:
- urgent or emergency based on neurologic/respiratory compromise

Immediate guidance:
- urgent same-day evaluation
- emergency pathway if severe neurologic or breathing signs


PROTOCOL 8: POSTPARTUM OR PREGNANCY RED FLAGS
==============================================
source_family: maternal_safety_pathway
source_version: MSP-2026.1

Red flags:
- heavy bleeding, severe headache with visual symptoms, shortness of breath, chest pain, unilateral leg swelling, severe abdominal pain

Triage:
- urgent to emergency based on severity

Immediate guidance:
- do not delay in-person care
- emergency services for severe bleeding, chest pain, breathing issues, neurologic symptoms


PROTOCOL 9: MENTAL HEALTH CRISIS
================================
source_family: mental_health_crisis_pathway
source_version: MHCP-2026.1

Red flags:
- active self-harm intent, suicidal thoughts with plan, severe agitation with safety risk

Triage:
- emergency/crisis intervention

Immediate guidance:
- contact emergency/crisis service immediately
- avoid staying alone during acute crisis


PROTOCOL 10: SELF-CARE ELIGIBLE COMMON SYMPTOMS
================================================
source_family: self_care_screen
source_version: SCS-2026.1

Examples:
- mild common cold symptoms without red flags
- mild stress symptoms without safety concerns
- routine sleep hygiene and nutrition questions

Triage:
- self_care

Guidance:
- brief practical recommendations
- escalation instructions if symptoms worsen or red flags appear
